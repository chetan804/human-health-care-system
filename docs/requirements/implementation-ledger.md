## Remaining provider-independent priorities

1. Identity-review/linking/merge governance, consent-purpose and guardian controls, configurable retention-policy lifecycle; do not choose a legal period or lawful basis by inference.
2. Notification routing/preference enforcement, retry/dead-letter/reconciliation; independent financial event/ledger/reconciliation and draft/dispute lifecycle without simulated settlement.
3. Complete organization/facility/staff/availability lifecycle and document/exchange scale/recovery controls; no production storage/scanner fallback.
4. Missing UI/component/all-role/error-state/critical-rule/performance/manual accessibility acceptance and operational hardening.

External contracts and clinical/legal approvals remain separate gates, not explanations for marking unfinished independent engineering complete. See `../operations/independent-workflows.md` for exact bounded behavior and `../testing/evidence/increment-0007/` for current results.
